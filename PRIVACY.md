# Privacy policy: NetSpeed Monitor Taskbar

*Last updated: 18 October 2026. Publisher: XCALT.*

NetSpeed Monitor Taskbar shows your upload and download speed in the Windows
taskbar. It does not collect, send or sell any data.

**What the app reads, on your own computer only**

- The byte counters Windows keeps for each network adapter, once per second,
  to work out the current speed.

**What the app writes**

- Its own settings (position, opacity, units) and today's download/upload
  totals in the app's local data folder.

**Network**

The app makes no network connections of its own and does not inspect the
content of your traffic. The **Speed test** button opens <https://fast.com> in
your browser, only when you click it.

**Contact**

Questions: open an issue at
<https://github.com/Arifedisant/NetSpeedBarWindows/issues>.

## Kebijakan privasi: NetSpeed Monitor Taskbar

NetSpeed Monitor Taskbar menampilkan kecepatan unggah dan unduh di taskbar
Windows. Aplikasi ini tidak mengumpulkan, mengirim, atau menjual data apa pun.

- **Yang dibaca (hanya di komputer sendiri):** penghitung byte tiap adaptor
  jaringan dari Windows, sekali per detik.
- **Yang ditulis:** pengaturan aplikasi dan total unduh/unggah hari ini di folder
  data lokal aplikasi.
- **Jaringan:** aplikasi tidak membuat koneksi sendiri dan tidak melihat isi lalu
  lintas. Tombol **Tes kecepatan** membuka <https://fast.com> hanya saat diklik.
- **Kontak:** buka issue di tautan GitHub di atas.
